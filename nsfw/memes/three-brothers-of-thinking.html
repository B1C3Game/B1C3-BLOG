The Three Brothers Of Thinking

Reasoning literacy poster comparing strawman, steelman, and evidence-first posture.

Definition

Reasoning quality improves when claims are separated into weak framing, strongest framing, and verified evidence.

When To Use

Use when debates are high-confidence but low-verification and you need to reset to evidence discipline.

Critical Reading Note

This piece uses intentionally sharp rhetorical framing. Treat it as inspectable doctrine content, not as a claim of moral superiority. Disagreement is expected and useful.
