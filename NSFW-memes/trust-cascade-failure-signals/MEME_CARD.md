# Meme Card: Trust Cascade Failure Signals

## Term

Trust Cascade Failure Signals

## One-line Definition

Once trust signal failures accumulate, institutional collapse becomes self-reinforcing.

## Full Definition

This piece maps how trust systems fail: individual signal failures (broken promises, inconsistent words/actions, hidden decisions) accumulate until the entire system cascades into distrust. The critique is on recognizing failure signals early, not on distrust itself.

## When To Use

- An institution is showing multiple small trust failures and you need to predict trajectory.
- People are dismissing individual failures as isolated when they're actually cascade precursors.
- You need to make the failure pattern visible before collapse.

## Not This

This is not a call for permanent distrust or anti-institution stance. It is a call for early recognition of failure cascade patterns and intervention before collapse becomes inevitable.

## Caption Options

- Small failures cascade into collapse.
- Watch the signals, not just the incidents.
- Intervention is cheaper than recovery.

## Post Hook

Institutional collapse rarely happens overnight. It follows predictable failure cascade patterns where individual signal failures accumulate until recovery becomes impossible. This meme suggests recognizing the pattern early.
