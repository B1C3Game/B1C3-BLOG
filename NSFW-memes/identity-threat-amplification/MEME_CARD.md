# Meme Card: Identity Threat Amplification

## Term

Identity Threat Amplification

## One-line Definition

Identity-based threat framing hardens group boundaries and makes cooperation harder.

## Full Definition

This piece shows how framing policy disagreements as identity threats triggers defensive group cohesion and reduces capacity for collaboration. The critique is on recognizing the rhetorical pattern, not on rejecting identity itself.

## When To Use

- A disagreement is being framed as existential threat to a group.
- You need to separate policy disagreement from identity threat.
- You need to depolarize without denying legitimate group interests.

## Not This

This is not a call to ignore identity or group interests. It is a call to distinguish between legitimate group protection and threat-inflation that hardens boundaries unnecessarily.

## Caption Options

- Not all disagreement is identity threat.
- Watch how threats are framed, not just named.
- Depolarization starts with separating policy from identity.

## Post Hook

When policy disagreements are framed as identity threats, groups become defensive and cooperation becomes harder. This meme suggests distinguishing between real threats and threat-inflation used for polarization.
