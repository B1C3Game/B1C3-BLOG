# Meme Card - Feelings Allowed, Residency Denied

## Term

Feelings Allowed, Residency Denied

## One-line Definition

Feel your anger, process it, then leave before it becomes your home.

## Urban Dictionary Style Meaning

This meme turns revenge rumination into an amusement park to expose how addictive grievance loops can feel. It validates emotional reality while setting a boundary: anger can visit, it cannot govern identity.

## When To Use

- A conflict keeps replaying in your head with imaginary arguments.
- A team needs permission to feel without normalizing pettiness loops.
- You want emotional honesty plus behavioral boundaries in one frame.

## Not This

Not emotional suppression and not moral superiority theater. This is processing guidance through satire.

## Caption Options

- Anger is welcome. Residency applications denied.
- Visit revenge land, do not move in.
- Feel it fully, do not build your identity there.

## Post Hook

Many people confuse emotional permission with emotional residence. This meme separates the two. You can acknowledge anger honestly while refusing to let rumination become your long-term operating system.
