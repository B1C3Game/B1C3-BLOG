# Meme Card: Genius As Process, Not Identity

## Term

Genius As Process, Not Identity

## One-line Definition

Insight quality rises when process quality rises.

## Full Definition

This poster reframes genius as a trainable five-step workflow:

1. Hold multiple chunks
2. Observe relations
3. Detect AHA transitions
4. Document with context
5. Transfer to others

The claim is procedural and verifiable. It avoids status hierarchy and superiority rhetoric.

## When To Use

- A conversation is stuck in identity labels instead of method quality.
- A team has insight moments but weak documentation and transfer.
- You need an education-first frame for cognitive process literacy.

## Not This

This is not "I am a genius" branding. This is "this process can be practiced" framing.

## Caption Options

- Do not worship the person. Audit the process.
- Process-state beats identity theater.
- No trace, no claim.

## Post Hook

People debate who counts as gifted while ignoring the workflow that creates insight. This poster shifts attention from identity performance to repeatable cognition under real constraints.
