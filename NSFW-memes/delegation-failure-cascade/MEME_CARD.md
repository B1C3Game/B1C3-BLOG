# Meme Card: Delegation Failure Cascade

## Term

Delegation Failure Cascade

## One-line Definition

Failure to properly delegate creates bottlenecks that cascade into system failure.

## Full Definition

This piece maps how insufficient delegation (whether from fear, distrust, or control needs) creates system bottlenecks. When one person or team becomes the constraint, the entire system stalls. The critique is on making delegation responsibility visible.

## When To Use

- A system is bottlenecked at a single person or small team.
- You need to show why lack of delegation causes failure.
- You need to make the delegation capacity visible as a leadership metric.

## Not This

This is not anti-leadership or a call for leaders to stop deciding. It is a call for leaders to distribute decision-making capacity, not concentrate it.

## Caption Options

- Bottlenecks are delegation failures.
- Watch who makes every decision.
- Delegation capacity is leadership capacity.

## Post Hook

When leaders cannot or will not delegate, systems become bottlenecked at the single decision-maker. This meme suggests that delegation capacity is core to leadership effectiveness.
