# Meme Card - Mirror Doctrine

## Term

Mirror Doctrine

## One-line Definition

When a system speaks in empathy slogans but operates as a control machine.

## Urban Dictionary Style Meaning

Mirror Doctrine is what happens when institutions market care but deliver compliance theater. It calls out environments where every sentence sounds humane, while every process is built to classify, manage, and normalize people into predefined boxes.

## When To Use

- When leadership says "nobody gets left behind" while excluding anyone outside the framework.
- When support language is mostly KPI dashboards and audit rituals.
- When policy teams celebrate inclusion while expanding behavioral control.

## Not This

Not anti-care. It is anti fake-care, where compassion is used as branding for stricter administration.

## Caption Options

- "Ingen lamnas utanfor. Ingen lamnas ifred."
- "Empati som process, kontroll som kultur."
- "When inclusion becomes a workflow prison."

## Post Hook

This meme is funny because it is too close to reality: institutions often confuse care with management. Mirror Doctrine makes the hidden trade explicit, language of empathy on top, machinery of control underneath.
