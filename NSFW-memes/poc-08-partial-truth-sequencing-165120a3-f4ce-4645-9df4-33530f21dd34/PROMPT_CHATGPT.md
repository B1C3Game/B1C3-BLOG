# Prompt: When The Whole Truth Cannot Land At Once

## Winning Prompt (V6 Civilian Translation)

```text
Recreate the exact four-section structure of the WHEN TRUTH NEEDS A SOFT LANDING poster in a civilian emergency context.

Keep exactly:
- Section 1: limited picture, high stakes (dispatcher / panicked caller scenario)
- Section 2: full picture held at command (dispatch sees active threats, safe routes, responding units)
- Section 3: why sequence matters (calm first, simple actions, position, then full truth)
- Section 4: the principle (partial truth can be the bridge to action)

Landing line (exact): PARTIAL TRUTH CAN BE THE BRIDGE TO ACTION.
Concept note footer (exact): Concept poster, not operational guidance.

Tone: serious, humane, precise. No military aesthetics, no propaganda read.
One high-resolution vertical poster only.
```

## Origin

This poster emerged from the B1C3-COMEDY-CORE session (2026-06-21).
It was not planned as the destination.
The session started with doctrine infographics, moved through office satire, family care scenes, and a military pilot scenario before landing on emergency dispatch as the strongest civilian frame.

## Key Decision

The civilian dispatcher context was chosen because:
- Stakes are undeniable without combat aesthetics
- Partial truth is operationally legible, not socially soft
- Dignity is preserved: the caller is treated as someone to protect, not deceive
- The concept note footer removes the "training advice" read
