# Meme Card - Anchor Test (Steelman)

## Term

Anchor Test (Steelman)

## One-line Definition

A doctrine-style checklist meme for testing whether extensions remain grounded.

## Urban Dictionary Style Meaning

This is the steelman form of the anchor meme. It moves from metaphor to method by adding explicit criteria. It calls out the habit of extending explanations without renewing empirical or decision anchors.

## When To Use

- You want to close a thread with a constructive framework.
- A discussion needs evaluative criteria, not just critique.
- You need a reusable doctrine artifact across domains.

## Not This

This is not a rejection of abstraction. It is a demand for explicit grounding checkpoints.

## Caption Options

- Anchor test: observable, testable, predictive, falsifiable, decision-relevant.
- Keep exploring, keep anchoring.
- Curiosity builds tools, anchors keep them honest.

## Post Hook

Critique without method is just style. This steelman turns the anchor metaphor into a practical filter teams can apply before committing to another layer of explanation.
