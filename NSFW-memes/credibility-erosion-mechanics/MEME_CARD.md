# Meme Card: Credibility Erosion Mechanics

## Term

Credibility Erosion Mechanics

## One-line Definition

Small credibility violations accumulate until total credibility loss becomes irreversible.

## Full Definition

This piece maps the path from small credibility violations to total credibility collapse: first violation → people notice → second violation confirms pattern → credibility erosion accelerates → total loss becomes inevitable. The critique is on preventing the pattern early, not on denying mistakes.

## When To Use

- An institution is showing small credibility violations and needs to recognize trajectory.
- You need to show why "one mistake" is actually a system failure.
- You need to make small credibility violations visible before they cascade.

## Not This

This is not a call for institutions never to make mistakes. It is a call for visible accountability when mistakes happen, so they don’t accumulate into credibility destruction.

## Caption Options

- Small violations accumulate into collapse.
- Watch the pattern, not the incident.
- Credibility destroyed is credibility lost.

## Post Hook

Institutional credibility is fragile. Small violations accumulate and accelerate toward total loss. This meme suggests that visible accountability for small failures prevents cascading into institutional collapse.
