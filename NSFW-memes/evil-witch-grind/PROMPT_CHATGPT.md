# ChatGPT Prompt - Evil Witch Grind

## Intent

Turn the concept into a mythic movie-poster tragedy: a person becomes collateral damage of her time, but her legacy unintentionally breaks the village's curse.

## Primary Prompt

Use this prompt in ChatGPT web image generation:

Create a darkly humorous medieval-fantasy movie poster with tragic grandeur and strange social commentary.
Core concept: a young woman was made into collateral damage by rumor, contradiction, and malign actors, but her obsessive introspection and strange legacy unintentionally uncursed the village that condemned her.
Scene: dramatic medieval alchemy chamber with heavy elixirs, broken seals, scrolls, apothecary glass, accusation records, and distant village lights beyond the window.
Main character: a scholar-witch figure, grave and intelligent, framed like a tragic protagonist whose private suffering outlived the smallness of her accusers.
Tone: mythic, dark, emotionally precise, satirical by implication, not melodramatic in a cheap way.
Main message text:
- EVIL WITCH GRIND
- "The collateral tragedy that unintentionally uncursed a village by legacy."
- "Her introspection changed a nation."
- "Her spirit haunts the unrighteous."
Visual language: premium film poster, heavy elixir symbolism, accusation as folklore, legacy as aftershock, clear focal character, ritual glass and scroll density, one memorable silhouette.
Color palette: parchment beige, midnight blue, candle amber, muted ink red.
Humor style: darkly deadpan, almost too serious, where the absurdity comes from treating social failure like epic folklore.
No real logos, no copyrighted fantasy IP, no hateful symbols, no explicit violence.
Output: one high-resolution web-ready poster image.

## Negative Constraints

- Do not glamorize self-harm or burnout.
- Do not mock neurodivergence or depict it as a defect.
- Keep irony legible and constructive.
- Avoid over-cluttered tiny text.
- No real person likenesses.

## Variant Prompts

### Variant A: Village Curse Poster

Same concept, but emphasize the village as spiritually diseased long before she was blamed.
Headline: "They named her curse. History named her cure."
Subline: "The village healed after condemning its mirror."

### Variant B: Solo Coder Altar

Same concept, but focus on the protagonist alone with elixirs, black candles, and pages of introspection as if conducting forbidden self-analysis.
Headline: "She drank every answer. The village kept asking the wrong question."
Subline: "Sometimes the cure arrives after the accusation."

### Variant C: Minimal Doctrine Poster

Minimal portrait + one torn scroll + one warning line.
Headline only: "Her legacy outlived their verdict."
