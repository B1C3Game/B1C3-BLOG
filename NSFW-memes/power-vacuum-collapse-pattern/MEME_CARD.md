# Meme Card: Power Vacuum Collapse Pattern

## Term

Power Vacuum Collapse Pattern

## One-line Definition

Power vacuums produce predictable collapse sequences when coordination mechanisms fail.

## Full Definition

This piece maps the mechanical progression of power system collapse: power concentrates → legitimate feedback mechanisms fail → coordination becomes impossible → collapse accelerates. The critique is on recognizing the pattern early, not on condemning power itself.

## When To Use

- A system is showing early-stage power concentration.
- You need to predict the collapse trajectory before it becomes inevitable.
- You need to show why feedback and transparency matter structurally.

## Not This

This is not anarchist or anti-power polemic. It is a call for structural safeguards that keep power systems from concentrating beyond recovery.

## Caption Options

- Concentration without feedback produces collapse.
- Watch the feedback mechanisms, not just the power.
- Collapse is predictable if you know the pattern.

## Post Hook

Power systems collapse in predictable patterns once concentration exceeds the system's feedback and coordination capacity. This meme suggests monitoring concentration and feedback mechanisms as early-warning indicators.
