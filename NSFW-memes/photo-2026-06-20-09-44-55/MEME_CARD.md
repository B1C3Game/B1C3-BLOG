# Meme Card - Schrodingers Ansvar

## Term

Schrodingers Ansvar

## One-line Definition

A situation where everyone participates in a decision but responsibility vanishes when consequences arrive.

## Urban Dictionary Style Meaning

Schrodingers Ansvar describes organizations that split ownership so widely that no one is accountable at the end. It mocks process-heavy teams where every task is delegated, reviewed, re-routed, and documented, yet final responsibility remains in superposition until failure lands.

## When To Use

- When six teams touched a project and all claim they were only supporting.
- When postmortems produce many action points but no clear accountable owner.
- When meetings optimize process optics while outcomes degrade.

## Not This

Not saying collaboration is bad. It is targeting accountability diffusion disguised as collaboration.

## Caption Options

- "Alla visste. Ingen agde. Nu loggas allt."
- "Delat ansvar, delad skuld, delad forsvinning."
- "Processen fungerade. Resultatet uteblev."

## Post Hook

This meme works because most teams have lived it: endless process activity without ownership closure. Schrodingers Ansvar turns that invisible pattern into one clear frame you can point to in seconds.
