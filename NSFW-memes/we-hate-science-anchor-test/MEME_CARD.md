# Meme Card - Where Is The Next Anchor

## Term

Where Is The Next Anchor

## One-line Definition

A meme about asking for grounding when theory keeps extending into uncertainty.

## Urban Dictionary Style Meaning

This meme calls out momentum without checkpoints. It applies to research, product strategy, AI discourse, and institutions that keep adding layers while forgetting to ask what reconnects claims to reality. It mocks drift, not curiosity.

## When To Use

- A discussion keeps expanding models without new validation anchors.
- A team confuses activity volume with grounded progress.
- You need to pause escalation and ask a decision-relevance question.

## Not This

This is not anti-science or anti-exploration. It is a process discipline reminder.

## Caption Options

- Exploration is good, but eventually you need the next anchor.
- Not anti-science, anti-unanchored claims.
- Progress without anchors becomes explanation theater.

## Post Hook

Most failures in complex systems are not from lack of effort. They come from drift, where extensions multiply faster than grounding checks. This meme makes one governance question unavoidable: where is the next anchor?
