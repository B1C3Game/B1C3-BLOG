# Meme Card: Institutional Apparatus Critique

## Term

Institutional Apparatus Critique

## One-line Definition

Institutional language normalizes power while appearing neutral and technical.

## Full Definition

This piece shows how apparatus-style framing (process-first, emotion-stripped, authority-centered) functions as a control mechanism while claiming administrative necessity.

The critique is not anti-administration. It is anti-invisible-power-through-language.

## When To Use

- A process is being justified as neutral when it actually serves interests.
- Language is being used to flatten human stakes into procedure.
- You need a visible contrast between human stakes and institutional framing.

## Not This

This is not anti-institution or anti-authority. It is a call for visible framing of power, not hidden power disguised as procedure.

## Caption Options

- Neutral language can be the most political.
- Process serves interests, not the reverse.
- Show the power, don't hide it in procedure.

## Post Hook

Institutions use language like a lens. When the lens claims to be neutral, power becomes invisible. This meme suggests making that power visible as a precondition for legitimate authority.
