# Meme Card - Doctrine Is Dormant Capability

## Term

Doctrine Is Dormant Capability

## One-line Definition

Doctrine is a standby toolset for assumption checks when reality shifts.

## Urban Dictionary Style Meaning

This meme calls out two failure modes at once: treating doctrine as decorative slogans and treating doctrine as panic-only emergency action. It reframes doctrine as routine operational discipline used when context changes.

## When To Use

- Teams keep executing old assumptions despite changing conditions.
- Decisions are defended by prestige instead of reality checks.
- You need to normalize re-examining assumptions without drama.

## Not This

Not a chaos button, not anti-principle. It is a permission mechanism for disciplined reassessment.

## Caption Options

- Doctrine is dormant capability, not decorative language.
- Use often, use wisely, not just when everything is burning.
- Nothing is sacred means assumptions are testable, not untouchable.

## Post Hook

Most systems fail slowly before they fail loudly. This meme encodes a healthier reflex: when context changes, re-open assumptions early, before prestige and sunk cost lock you into outdated moves.
