# Meme Card: Structure Is Not Neutral

## Term

Structure Is Not Neutral

## One-line Definition

Every structure preserves some information and destroys other information.

## Full Definition

When you impose structure on reality you gain consistency, inspectability, and repeatability. You also lose hesitation, emotional weighting, narrative context, and the reason you asked in the first place.

This is not a failure of structure. It is the nature of structure.

The question is: what information did this structure make expensive to see?

## Core Claim

The frame that produces the prompt changes the image.

- Direct conversation: includes tone, recurring themes, implicit priorities
- Structured dataprompt: gains consistency, loses spontaneity and narrative context
- Reverse-engineered prompt: is not recovery, it is a new interpretation that drifts

Same underlying reality. Different frame. Different visible information. Different output.

## Examples

| Structure | What It Preserves | What It Destroys |
|-----------|------------------|-----------------|
| Slug | Stable URL identity | Narrative and context |
| Registry | Organization and linkage | Content meaning |
| Prompt template | Repeatability | Spontaneity and emotional weight |
| SPOC | Coordination | Perspective diversity |

None of these are automatically bad.
The question is always: what is now expensive to see?

## When To Use

Use this meme when:
- Someone treats a structure as if it is neutral or complete
- A process creates invisible information loss
- A SPOC or single model is filtering reality before it reaches the organization
- Reverse-engineered prompts or templates produce drift nobody can explain
- The structure makes failure invisible rather than legible

## Doctrine Link

Related to Evidence Man doctrine: "Audit the method, not the mythology."

The structure is the method. Structure determines what evidence can be gathered. Different structure, different visible evidence, different conclusion.

## Not An Endorsement Of

This meme does not argue against structure. It argues for awareness of what each structure costs.
